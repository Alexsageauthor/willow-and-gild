WHAT IS NEW
  Two more buttons beside "Download seating chart":
      Download Find Your Seat
      Download place cards
  Both are drawn from the SAME guest list already typed into the planner.
  Enter your guests once and every product that names them comes out filled.

  NOTHING IS LOCKED TO A TABLE SIZE. A table of six yields six place cards,
  not six and four blanks, and its Find Your Seat block is six rows tall.
  Blank guest boxes are skipped entirely.

WHY IT IS HERE AND NOT IN THE PDFs
  A PDF form can copy a value into every field sharing its name - that is how
  the place-card master list already fills all eighty cards. What it cannot do
  is COMPACT a list, because that needs computation, and PDF JavaScript is
  ignored by Preview, by Chrome and by every phone. Sharing field names across
  products would only have worked by fixing the seats per table.
  This page can compact. So this is where it belongs.

  The fillable PDFs are UNCHANGED. Anyone who would rather type into the
  document still can.
