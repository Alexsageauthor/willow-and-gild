The Collections

Fifteen worlds, each complete

A collection is not a matching set of three cards. It is every piece of paper a wedding needs, drawn together — and it is finished before it goes on sale.

Pick & mix

Nothing here is all or nothing

Buy a whole collection, one chapter of it, or a single card. Mix designs within a collection — six table numbers, five place cards, three menus, choose the ones you like. And mix across collections too: Kyoto invitations with Olive & Gold signage is a decision, not a mistake. Every collection shares the same paper, the same measures and the same fillable fields, so pieces sit together whichever way you combine them.

Type it once

It fills the whole suite

Your names, your date, your venue — entered on one sheet and carried through every page that needs them. No retyping, nothing to mistype twice.

Yours to change

Every line is editable

Not just the blanks. Rename a course, a reading or a heading, or clear a row you don't need and it disappears — so the paper fits your day rather than a template.

Print anywhere
