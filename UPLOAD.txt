WILLOW & GILD - THE SEATING CHART BUILDER COULD NOT EXPORT

WHAT WAS WRONG
  seating-planner.html fetches six files from assets/ and embeds them into the
  PDF: Chorus.ttf, Pagella.ttf, Pagella-Italic.ttf, branch.png,
  divider_fan.png, paper.jpg.

  NONE OF THE SIX WAS IN THE REPO. assets/ held 168 files and not one of them.
  A 404 returns Vercel's HTML error page as bytes, embedFont throws on it, and
  the catch in downloadPDF() shows "Something went wrong building the PDF" -
  to a customer who had paid, entered their access code and designed their
  whole chart. The preview worked, so nothing looked broken until the last
  click.

WHAT IS IN HERE
  assets/Chorus.ttf            from fonts/ - exact
  assets/Pagella.ttf           from fonts/ - exact
  assets/Pagella-Italic.ttf    from fonts/ - exact
  assets/paper.jpg             850 x 1100, the exact size the code expects
  assets/branch.png            Kyoto's cascading blossom branch, portrait
  assets/divider_fan.png       Kyoto's floral rule, 1833 x 158
  seating-planner.html         aspect ratios no longer hardcoded

  The planner's palette is ink #3F4A3C and gold #B7995C, which is Kyoto's
  exactly, so Kyoto art is the right choice. branch.png and divider_fan.png
  are my substitutions - the originals are in neither the repo nor the
  archive and appear never to have existed. PLEASE LOOK AT AN EXPORTED PDF
  AND TELL ME IF THEY ARE RIGHT.

  The code also hardcoded three aspect ratios (708/1048, 9.536, 850x1100), so
  any replacement art would have been silently stretched. It now reads the
  real size off each embedded image.

HOW TO UPLOAD
  Repo-root relative. 6 files into assets/, and seating-planner.html at the
  root over the existing one. 7 files.

THEN TEST
  Open the planner, enter the access code, add a table or two with names,
  and press Download print-ready PDF. It should produce a poster instead of
  an error.
