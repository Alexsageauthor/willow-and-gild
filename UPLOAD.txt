ONE FILE: collection.html, repo root, over the existing one.

WHAT WAS ACTUALLY WRONG
  Not the gap. The dots are <img> elements inside .hero, so they were picking
  up this rule:
      .hero img{width:min(360px,62vw);margin:0 auto clamp(20px,3vw,36px)}
  `width` was already beaten on specificity by .palette .dot. MARGIN WAS NOT.
  An auto margin on a flex item absorbs every pixel of free space in the line,
  which overrides justify-content:center and makes `gap` almost meaningless.

  Measured off the Woodlands screenshot: 44px dots sitting 96px apart, while
  the CSS said gap:13px. That is why changing 16 to 13 did nothing - the gap
  was never what was spacing them. The same rule's bottom margin was also
  pushing the dots down the page.

  The fix is `margin:0` on .palette .dot. The three dots will now sit 13px
  apart, centred, 158px wide in total.
