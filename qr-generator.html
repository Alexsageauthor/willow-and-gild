How to use this on your QR card

1

Enter your link above and download the PNG

For a printed card, 400 px is plenty — it will be sharp at any size a card would reasonably be. For a sign that guests read from across a room, use 600 or 800 px.

2

Open your fillable PDF in Adobe Acrobat Reader

Open the *_Fillable.pdf version of your QR card. You will see a box marked your QR here. Click it and choose Place Image, then select the PNG you just downloaded.

3

Test it before you print

Save the PDF, open your phone's camera and point it at the screen. The link should open. If it does not, check that the URL you entered is correct and try again. Only print once the QR scans reliably.

4

Print at actual size

In Acrobat, choose Print → Actual Size (not Fit to Page). Your print test sheet in folder 28_Print_Shop_Guide will confirm the sizing is right before you commit to a full run.

QR codes work best when the destination URL is short and stable. If your wedding website address is very long, consider a free redirect service like bit.ly to shorten it first — shorter URLs produce less dense, easier-to-scan QR codes.
