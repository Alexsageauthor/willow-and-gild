WILLOW & GILD - FORCED DARK MODE OPT-OUT, ALL 9 PAGES
(also includes the collection-page dot spacing fix)

WHAT CHANGED
  Every page now declares  <meta name="color-scheme" content="only light">
  and                      html{color-scheme:only light;background:#F8F6F2}

  Two differences from before:
   1. The keyword order. The documented opt-out is "only light". The site said
      "light only" on the four pages that declared anything at all.
   2. Five pages declared NOTHING: find-your-palette, free-timeline, palette,
      seating-planner, tile-check. They do now.
   3. html carries an explicit opaque background. Chromium ignores TRANSPARENT
      backgrounds when deciding whether to auto-darken, and only bundle.html
      had one.

  collection.html also has the palette dot gap at 13px instead of 16px, which
  is the same 30% of the dot width the home page uses.
